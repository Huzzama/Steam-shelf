# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-06

First release. Windows and Linux.

- Burn one CD/DVD per game you own on Steam; put the disc in and the game starts
  (or its store page opens). A countdown card first, cancel if you want.
- Never launches a game at boot or wake-up: only a disc *inserted* while the PC is on.
- Game mode (background agent, starts when you log in) and burner mode (the
  window is open: discs are left alone).
- Test with a virtual disc, without burning anything. "Simulate the disc going in".
- Burning: IMAPI2 on Windows (what Explorer uses), xorriso on Linux. Erase and
  reuse rewritable discs; point any disc to another game.
- Third-party games (GOG, Epic, emulators…): add them to Steam, then burn them
  like any other.
- Steam Family: members' games count as yours to burn, tagged on the profile.
- Covers: your own art in Steam (Grunge Editor) first, Steam's library cache,
  Steam's official art; SteamGridDB (your key) for third-party games.
- Your Steam Shelf on your pimpmysteam.com profile: every game that reached a
  disc, with your own covers (60 per account; Ko-fi supporters: unlimited).
  Free accounts show 100 games; you pick which.
- Steam opens the game on the desktop or in Big Picture (Settings).
- 26 languages.
